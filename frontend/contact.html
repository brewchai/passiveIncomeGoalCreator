Frequently Asked Questions

Who am I actually talking to?

Me, Ninad. One person, no team and no company behind the curtain. I'm an ex-Meta engineer who went from $40,000 in debt to financially independent at 33, and I now live as an NRI nomad in Da Nang, Vietnam. Every calculator, article, and reply here comes from me.

Can you give me personalized financial advice?

I'm not a licensed financial advisor, so I won't give formal advice or tell you which specific stocks or funds to buy. What I can do is get on a call and walk through your actual situation with real numbers and lived experience, which is often exactly what people are missing. If you want that, book a call.

Can you actually help with my situation as an NRI?

Most likely, yes, because that's a lot of who I work with. People on visas in the US, NRIs planning their money around India, and anyone trying to make the geo-arbitrage math work. I lived across New York, Seattle, Austin, and Orlando before leaving, so the US-to-abroad path is one I have actually walked, not just read about.

How accurate are the retirement projections?

They are estimates, not promises. Each one runs on assumptions like a chosen return, inflation, and withdrawal rate, and real markets, taxes, and life will always behave differently. Use them to understand the shape of your plan, then stress-test it against a bad early market, which is the risk most people never account for.
